What is Ocean P3?

Ocean P3 is a Pick 3 lottery number suggestion app that provides daily number recommendations based on historical patterns. Numbers are provided for informational purposes only.

Subscription

Ocean P3 offers a monthly subscription at $7.99/month with a 7-day free trial. Billing is handled through the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel anytime.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOSSettings → your name → Subscriptions → Ocean P3 → Cancel
AndroidGoogle Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Ocean P3 → Cancel

Refunds

Refunds are handled directly by Apple or Google, not by us:

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

Restoring Purchases

Open Ocean P3 and tap Restore Purchase on the subscription screen while signed into the same account used for your original purchase.

Technical Issues

Check your internet connection and try closing and reopening the app. If the issue continues, contact us with your device type, OS version, and a description of the issue.

Contact
