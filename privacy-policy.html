Ocean P3

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026  ·  Developer: Seron Moore

Overview

Ocean P3 is a Pick 3 lottery number suggestion app. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

We are committed to handling your data with transparency and care. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to operate the app:

  • Subscription and purchase data — managed by RevenueCat, our subscription platform. This includes your subscription status, purchase history, and an anonymous user identifier. RevenueCat does not collect your payment details; those are handled entirely by Apple or Google.
  • App usage data — we use Firebase Firestore solely to retrieve Pick 3 number data displayed in the app. We do not store personal information in Firestore.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

How We Use Information

The information collected is used only to:

  • Verify your subscription status and unlock app features
  • Restore purchases if you reinstall the app or switch devices
  • Deliver Pick 3 number content from our database

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

Third-Party Services

Ocean P3 uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • RevenueCat — subscription management. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing. Your payment details are handled exclusively by Apple or Google and are never shared with us.
  • Google Firebase Firestore — database for app content. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Data Retention

RevenueCat retains purchase and subscription records for as long as your account is active or as required to resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. We do not independently store this data.

Children's Privacy

Ocean P3 is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data associated with your use of the app. Because we do not directly store personal information beyond what RevenueCat manages, most requests should be directed to RevenueCat at revenuecat.com/privacy.

For any other privacy request, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
